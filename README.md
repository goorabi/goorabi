## About

I am an **Associate Professor at the Faculty of Geography, University of Tehran**, specializing in geomorphology and Earth-surface analysis. My research integrates **geomorphology, morphotectonics, remote sensing, GIS, InSAR, and spatial analysis** to investigate landscape evolution, active tectonics, ground deformation, environmental change, and geohazards.

A current research emphasis is **land subsidence**, particularly its spatial and temporal patterns, environmental controls, and geomorphological consequences, investigated through multi-temporal Earth observation and geospatial analysis.

## Current Research Focus

My current work emphasizes the integration of multi-temporal Earth-observation data with geomorphological and GIS-based analysis. Key themes include deformation time-series interpretation, land-subsidence assessment, morphotectonic analysis, and spatial investigation of relationships among geohazards, environmental conditions, exposed elements, and landscape sensitivity.

## Research Portfolio

This profile is being developed as a curated portfolio of **reproducible geoscience research**. Public repositories will document selected work in:

- InSAR and land-subsidence analysis
- Geomorphology and morphotectonic analysis
- GIS-based spatial hazard and risk assessment
- Sentinel-1 deformation time-series workflows
- DEM-based geomorphometric analysis
- Reproducible Python workflows for geoscience

Repository-level documentation will distinguish **data provenance, processing methodology, analytical code, derived outputs, and reproducibility requirements** wherever those materials can be released publicly.

## Open & Reproducible Research

I support research workflows that are **transparent, reproducible, spatially explicit, and scientifically defensible**. Where publication, ethical, and data-licensing conditions permit, repositories on this profile will provide documented code, analytical workflows, metadata, and selected research outputs that facilitate verification, reuse, and scientific collaboration.

> **Data policy:** Unpublished, confidential, restricted, student-sensitive, or third-party datasets are not made public. Public repositories contain only material appropriate for open scientific dissemination.
